# Platform, windows, cadence, dropped controls

Summary of manuscript Sections 3.1–3.2 and the post-level metadata paragraph in Section 3.

## Platform and inclusion
- All 1,012 posts collected from **Facebook only** (public pages/profiles).
- Comment panel switched to **All comments** (not Most relevant).
- **View more comments** clicked until exhausted; text/metadata read from DOM.
- Private messages, closed groups, and non-public replies not collected.

## Calendar alignment
- Study span: **32 calendar weeks (20 January–26 August 2026)**.
- Individual VIs contribute shorter overlapping windows (11–32 weeks).
- Only Tiểu Mỹ and Lâm Khả Hân observed from week 4; creation dates for three later channels unknown → cross-VI comparisons concern **levels**, not rates, of normalization.

## Dropped post-level controls (914/1,012 posts lack usable captions)
Available fields used in modelling: post identity, VI channel, calendar/`week_num`, media type (reel vs mixed).

Dropped (could not code full sample): `sponsored`, `ai_disclosure`, `realism`.

Plausible bias:
- Omitting **realism** may attribute some decline in perceptual resistance to habituation when part is improving graphics.
- Omitting **ai_disclosure** may attribute some rise in AI-nature criticism to within-VI dynamics when part is uneven disclosure salience.
- Omitting **sponsored** may blur commercial discourse prompted by ads versus spontaneous acceptance.
