# Human–human inter-coder reliability (revised instrument)

- **Items:** 200 shared comments, coded independently by two human coders on the revised coding sheet (definitions permanently visible, construct-without-aspect and `S_parasocial`-without-social-cue objections, mandatory 40-item calibration).
- **Coders:** Coder 1 = Minh (`gold2_chung_minh.csv`), Coder 2 = Trúc (`gold2_chung_truc.csv`).
- **Statistic:** Krippendorff's α per label (`intercoder_alpha_n200.csv`).

## Diagnostic note on `S_parasocial`

Disagreement is one-sided: Coder 1 = 1 and Coder 2 = 0 on 102 comments, the reverse on 1 (`S_parasocial_disagreement.csv`). Positive rates are 63.0% vs 12.5%. Because the two coders differ systematically rather than randomly, the manuscript does not treat these labels as passing and relies on human–model and cross-model agreement (Table 2), with caveats at each affected claim (Sections 4.3–4.5 and 5.8).
